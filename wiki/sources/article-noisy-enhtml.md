Field measurements across Bangkok's Ladprao district show a mean link budget of 138 dB at SF9, with packet delivery degrading sharply beyond 1.2 km when street-level nodes lack line of sight.

The gateway elevation mattered more than spreading factor: a 12th-floor rooftop node recovered 94 percent delivery where a 3rd-floor node achieved only 61 percent on the identical path.

Repeating through one intermediate hop restored delivery above 90 percent for both placements, at the cost of doubled airtime and a halved effective throughput budget.
